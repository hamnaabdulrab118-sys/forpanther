# Palms by night

A Pen created on CodePen.

Original URL: [https://codepen.io/ro-ka/pen/pwrRMG](https://codepen.io/ro-ka/pen/pwrRMG).

At night at the beach, looking up to the stars, some palm trees are blowing in the wind.

The stars are generated within WebGL. The palms come from a photograph (https://roka.photography/photo/night-sky/) by me from Malaysia in 2014. They get animated based on a depth map within WebGL.
